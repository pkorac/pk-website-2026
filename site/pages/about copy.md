**My core offer is a 7-week AI Product Sprint:** from idea to a working alpha/MVP.  
It works because I combine strategic product leadership with hands-on prototyping and an engineering-minded approach to design – so we can move fast and stay grounded in reality.

---

## Why this works

AI products fail in predictable ways: unclear use cases, fuzzy data assumptions, demos that don’t survive real users, and “chatbots” that never become real workflows.

My approach is prototype-first and outcome-led. Slides need to answer a different question for every person in the room. A realistic prototype answers many at once: interaction model, data needs, edge cases, trust and guardrails, and what “good” looks like. It turns opinions into something we can test – quickly.

I’ve spent 15+ years building human-centred tools and services across enterprise and consumer contexts, and more recently designing agentic products where trust, control surfaces, and observability matter as much as the chat experience.

## What I do

### Product leadership with technical depth

I’m comfortable in the messy middle: aligning stakeholders, setting direction, making trade-offs – and turning those into artefacts a team can actually build.

### Prototype-led decision making

I lead end-to-end prototyping that reduces ambiguity and accelerates validation. Sometimes that’s high-fidelity interactive prototypes. Sometimes it’s fully functional proof-of-concept builds. The goal is the same: make the decision obvious.

### AI with guardrails

I care about trustworthy AI: source grounding, clear boundaries, permissions, auditability, and “what happens when it goes wrong” are product features, not afterthoughts.
