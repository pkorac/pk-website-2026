# About

I'm a product and tech leader helping teams design and build AI products that ship – not just strategies that sound good in a deck.

**My core offer is a 7-week AI Product Sprint:** from idea to a working alpha/MVP.  
It works because I combine strategic product leadership with hands-on prototyping and an engineering-minded approach to design – so we can move fast and stay grounded in reality.

Alongside sprints, I work with small number of teams as a senior product designer and creative technologist – especially when rapid prototyping and applied AI are central to the work.

## Beyond the screen

Outside of work I’m a dog dad of two beautiful whippets and spend a lot of time on trails and fells. It turns out moving uphill for fun is decent training for shipping products under constraints. Read my Istria ultramarathon story to get a flavour of what I get up to.

## My approach

AI products fail in predicatble ways: unclear or forced use-cases, fuzzy data assumptions and demos that don't survive real users.

My approach is outcome led. **I believe in prototyping from the start, learning through making and that AI isn't just a chat-bot.**

I’ve spent 15+ years building human-centred tools and services across enterprise and consumer contexts, and more recently designing agentic products where trust, control surfaces, and observability matter as much as the the client experience.

## How I work

- **Fast, focused cycles** – tangible progress each week.
- **Small, senior teams** – fewer handoffs, better communication, more momentum.
- **Clarity over theatre** – prototypes over decks; decisions over vibes.
- **Pragmatic evaluation** – test early, measure what matters, iterate fast.
