## DevRev – Computer

GenUI-first AI teammate – conversation, but with real interfaces and real control

Computer is DevRev’s AI teammate: a conversational layer that unifies team context, answers questions, summarises work, and can automate actions – designed to feel consumer-grade in polish, but enterprise-grade in trust.

- **Client:** DevRev
- **Format:** Product work (10-month build)

- **Team:** 2 product designers, 2–3 engineers
- **Status:** closed beta, working towards GA

## The problem

Work is fragmented across tools, tickets, docs, and messages. People waste time hunting for context, repeating updates, and doing “coordination work” instead of actual work. Computer’s promise is to pull that context together so teams can move faster and make better decisions. [oai_citation:1‡DevRev](https://devrev.ai/meet-computer?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## The key insight – chat alone isn’t enough

A pure chat interface is fast for asking questions, but it breaks down when people need to _inspect_, _compare_, _verify_, or _take action_. The experience needed to combine conversation with **generated UI components** (tables, summaries, structured views) so users can trust what they’re seeing and act on it.

## What we designed and built

- **A conversational interface with GenUI components** – responses that “snap” into structured UI when needed (not just text)
- **Transparency surfaces** (e.g. “thinking stack”) to make agent behaviour more legible and auditable
- **Text-to-SQL workflows** to query enterprise data safely and return inspectable results
- **Integrations + memory foundation** via DevRev’s connector layer (AirSync) to keep enterprise data in sync for reliable context [oai_citation:2‡DevRev](https://devrev.ai/how-computer-works?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **A high-fidelity prototype-to-production loop** – using realism and polish to remove ambiguity for stakeholders and speed iteration

## Outcomes

- DevRev reports early adopters seeing results such as **10+ hours saved per employee weekly**, **up to 85% of customer issues resolved automatically**, and **~50% support cost reduction** (reported by DevRev for early adopters of Computer, not universal guarantees). ## What I learned

- GenUI isn’t a gimmick – it’s how you make AI behaviour _inspectable_ for real work.
- Trust comes from control surfaces: explainability, permissions, and “what happened when”.
- The best AI teammate experiences are multi-modal – chat is the entry point, not the whole product.
