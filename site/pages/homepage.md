## AI Product sprint

AI Product Sprint is the methodology I’ve refined over several years across real projects to help teams strategically design and build an AI product quickly – without falling into “design theatre”.

**In 7 weeks, you’ll have:**

- A working **alpha/MVP prototype** (demo-ready, testable, not slideware)
- A clear **interaction & governance model** (what the AI does, when, and how humans stay in control)
- The right **guardrails** (provenance, tone, permissions, failure modes)
- A practical **plan to ship** (roadmap, risks, and next-phase build plan)

**Ideal for teams who:**

- have a real workflow to improve (not just “let’s add a chatbot”)
- need alignment across stakeholders
- want something tangible to test with users quickly

### FAQs

- “We don’t have clean data yet” – we’ll prototype with what you have and define what you actually need.
- “We need it to be safe” – guardrails and observability are part of the deliverable.
- “We’re not sure what to build” – Week 0–1 is for narrowing to the highest-leverage workflow.
