# Re-imagining peterkoraca.com

One clear offer, supported by proof and ideas

Website needs to do 3 jobs:

1. Make the offer legible in 10 seconds
2. Prove you can deliver it (case studies, testimonials, outcomes, artefacts)
3. Show how you think (writing that attracts your ideal buyers)

Everything else becomes secondary.
Make the AI sprint the product, and leadership the proof. Not the other way round.

## Sitemap

- Work (case-studies)
- Blog (writing)
- About (credibility, how I work)
- Lab (experiments + art)
- Now (what i'm up to right now)

---

## Key principles & guidelines

- One primary CTA, one secondary path
   - **Primary CTA everywhere:** Book a Sprint Scoping Call
   - **Secondary (quiet but visible):** Need product/tech leadership? (links to a “Leadership engagements” section/page)

- Reframe your “About” as “Why this works”. Instead of “here’s my biography”, make it:
   - I’ve led X kinds of teams/products
   - I build and ship, not just advise
   - That’s why the sprint is low-risk and high-output

- Case studies categorised by outcomes, not job titles. For each case study, lead with the transformation:
   - “From scattered docs to answerable evidence in weeks”
   - “From concept to working agent prototype with evaluation plan”

- Positioning
   - **Line 1 (what you sell):** 7-week AI Product Sprint – idea to working alpha/MVP.
   - **Line 2 (why trust you):** Led and shipped AI products across teams – product strategy + engineering execution.

## “Other work”

Create a small page or section called something like “Other ways to work with me” with only 2 options:

- Fractional product/tech leadership (for teams who want ongoing direction after a sprint)
- Advisory / coaching (short, contained)

Crucially: frame these as adjacent to the sprint, not competing with it:

- “Often teams do a sprint first, then bring me in fractionally to scale it.”

Add a short “Is this you?” qualifier on the Sprint page:

- “You have a high-value AI opportunity, need something real in 7 weeks, and want a leader who can ship.”
