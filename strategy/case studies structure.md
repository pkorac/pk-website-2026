# Structure

## The backbone (every case study)

1. Outcome headline (transformation, ideally with a number)
2. At-a-glance: client, duration, team, your role, what shipped
3. Problem: what was broken/slow/risky
4. Approach: the key bet(s) you made and why
5. What we built: 3–6 bullets, with 1–3 visuals
6. Outcomes: metrics where possible, otherwise concrete signals
7. What I’d do next: shows leadership judgement
8. CTA: “If you’re facing X, the 7-week sprint is designed for it.”

## Plug-in modules (use as needed)

- Sprint timeline (Week 0–7) – for Yohana/CHI/UMI
- Agent trust & control – for Agent Studio/CHI/Computer
- Information architecture / interaction model pivot – for Tallis/Slack
- System/data model – for Yohana/CHI/UMI
- Shipping constraints (NDA, security, messy data, stakeholders) – anywhere it applies
